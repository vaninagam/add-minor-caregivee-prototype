Where to find this information
Look at the label on your child's prescription bottle. You'll find the Rx number near the top of the label, usually prefixed with "Rx#" or a barcode.

❌ If none of these work
If we're unable to verify your child's records through any of these methods, they cannot be added at this time. You may visit a CVS Pharmacy location for in-person assistance.
🏷️
Reading the Rx label
The Rx number is printed on every CVS prescription label and is unique to each filled prescription.
  • Located near the top of the label
  • Usually 10–13 digits
  • May include a dash (e.g. 4782901-01234)
  • Name must match the child's name exactly
